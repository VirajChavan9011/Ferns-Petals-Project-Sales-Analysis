My comprehensive sales analysis of the Ferns & Petals dataset successfully transformed raw transaction records into actionable business intelligence. The interactive dashboard reveals a strong total revenue of ₹35,20,984.00, indicating healthy market engagement. We determined that the average revenue per order stands at a solid ₹3,520.98, with customers typically purchasing about three items per transaction. Delivery logistics currently show an average delivery time of 5.53 days, providing a clear baseline metric for future supply chain optimization. By breaking down revenue by occasion, we identified that Anniversaries drive the highest sales at over ₹6.74 Lakhs, closely followed by Raksha Bandhan and general occasions. From a category perspective, "Colors" heavily dominates consumer interest, generating over ₹10 Lakhs in revenue and significantly outpacing Soft Toys and Sweets. Geographically, the data highlights strong demand in emerging regions, with Imphal, Dhanbad, and Kavali leading as the top three cities by order volume. Our analysis of individual items pinpointed the top revenue-generating products, led by the Magnam Set and Quia Gift. Temporal analysis yielded crucial insights into customer purchasing habits throughout the year. We observed major seasonal revenue spikes in February and August, which correlate perfectly with Valentine's Day and Raksha Bandhan respectively. Furthermore, evaluating hourly order data revealed that customer purchasing activity peaks at 19:00 (7 PM), identifying the absolute optimal daily window for targeted digital marketing. The integration of dynamic filters for order day, occasion, and gender allows stakeholders to deeply customize these insights on the fly. Ultimately, this analysis provides the marketing and operations teams with a data-driven foundation to optimize inventory planning, tailor advertising campaigns, and improve overall customer satisfaction.
