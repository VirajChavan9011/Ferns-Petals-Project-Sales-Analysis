This sales analysis project for FNP (Ferns and Petals) provides a comprehensive business overview of revenue performance, customer purchasing behavior, product demand, and operational efficiency. The dashboard reveals a total revenue of over ₹35 lakh, with an average order value of ₹3,520 and an average delivery time of 5.53 days, indicating strong customer spending and an efficient delivery network.

The analysis highlights that occasions such as Anniversary, Raksha Bandhan, and Holi generated the highest revenue, demonstrating the significant impact of seasonal and event-based marketing on sales growth. Product categories like Colors, Soft Toys, and Sweets emerged as top revenue contributors, helping identify high-performing inventory segments. Additionally, products such as Magnam Set and Quia Gift were among the top-selling items, reflecting customer preferences and gifting trends.

City-wise analysis showed that Imphal, Dhanbad, and Kavali recorded the highest order volumes, enabling the business to identify strong regional markets for targeted promotions and logistics optimization. Monthly sales trends indicated peak revenue periods during festive seasons, emphasizing the importance of demand forecasting and inventory planning.

Overall, this project demonstrates how data analytics can support strategic business decisions, improve customer experience, optimize operations, and maximize profitability in the gifting and e-commerce industry.
