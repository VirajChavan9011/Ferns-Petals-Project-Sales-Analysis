This project focuses on analyzing sales performance and customer behavior for FNP (Ferns and Petals), a gifting company specializing in occasion-based products such as Diwali gifts, Valentine’s Day hampers, birthday presents, and anniversary products. The objective of the analysis was to generate business insights that can help improve sales strategies, customer satisfaction, and operational efficiency.

Using data analysis and dashboard visualization techniques, the project examined key business metrics including total revenue, monthly sales trends, top-performing products, customer spending behavior, delivery performance, and city-wise order distribution. The analysis identified seasonal sales spikes during major occasions, highlighting the strong impact of festive demand on revenue generation. It also revealed the company’s highest revenue-generating products and the top cities contributing the most orders.

Customer spending patterns and product popularity by occasion provided valuable insights for targeted marketing and inventory planning. Additionally, the relationship between order quantity and delivery time helped evaluate operational efficiency and logistics performance.

From a business perspective, the project supports data-driven decision-making by helping management optimize product offerings, improve delivery operations, strengthen customer engagement, and design effective promotional campaigns during peak seasons. Overall, the analysis demonstrates how business intelligence can enhance profitability and customer experience in the retail gifting industry.
